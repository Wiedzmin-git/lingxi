# Lingxi · 靈犀

**Understanding through shared thought and action, between people and AI.**

Lingxi is an independent fork of [OpenCode](https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode), built on its V2 codebase. Our direction is a dependable workspace for sustained human–AI collaboration: persistent conversations, explicit execution state, communication between independent sessions, and language-aware work.

## The name

靈犀 (simplified: 灵犀; Mandarin pinyin: **Língxī**) evokes a subtle connection of understanding. A famous couplet by the Tang-dynasty poet **Li Shangyin (李商隱, ninth century)** reads:

> 身無彩鳳雙飛翼，心有靈犀一點通。

In a free rendering: *Our bodies have no paired wings of a brilliant phoenix; our hearts are joined by a fine thread of understanding.*

The original image draws on a traditional belief in a connecting line inside a rhinoceros horn. The poem is love poetry. Our use of that image for human–AI collaboration is a contemporary interpretation, not a claim that the ancient expression referred to artificial intelligence. Understanding here includes clarification, disagreement, and a willingness to revise one's view.

Source: Li Shangyin, **無題**, beginning **昨夜星辰昨夜風** ([text and commentary](https://www.diando.net/shiwen/wu-ti-zuo-ye-xing-chen-zuo-ye-feng)). The spelling above uses traditional characters; the pronunciation is modern Mandarin.

## Development status

The fork is being established from upstream commit `0fd7e2829449b052abf0078666669302923d77af` (OpenCode 2.0.23), with locally developed changes being imported and verified. See [Lingxi development notes](DEVELOPMENT.lingxi.md) for the fork's build boundary; the existing upstream README describes the inherited project.

Planned delivery uses `dev` and `stable` branches and private Windows release channels. Automatic update recovery, the Lingxi-branded installer, and the integrated language-service interface are not yet released. A branch name alone is not a tested release.
